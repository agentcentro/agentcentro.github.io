A personal note

Please leave everyone mentioned in this documentary alone. Please don’t contact them about the match, join their games because of this story, or encourage anyone else to harass them.

My hands are shaking a little as I write this. I’d had a long day of people trying to get a reaction out of me online. I was bored and feeling pretty empty, so I joined Mase and Gilbert. I thought playing with them for a while might help me settle down.

I thought we’d agreed to use energy rifles only. Then I found myself in a two-on-one, and we weren’t playing Juggernaut. I asked Gilbert, “Energy only or what?” He said no. When I brought up what I remembered us agreeing to, he said, “My life, my rules.” I felt frustrated because I’d gone into the match expecting all three of us to play by the same rule.

I left the game to cool off. For a moment I wanted to unfriend them, which says a lot about how wound up I felt by then. What I needed most that night was a break.
